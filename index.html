What this website is for

The Student Event Hub was built to replace manual event sign-ups and paper attendance sheets used during seminars, orientations, workshops, and cultural programs at CCSJDM. Student organizations publish their events here, students reserve a slot online, and attendance is confirmed at the venue using the student's ID number.

📅

Discover Events

Browse every approved campus event in one directory. Filter by category, organization, or schedule so you only see what is relevant to you.

📝

Reserve a Slot

Fill out a short registration form to secure your seat. Slot limits are enforced automatically, so an event can never be overbooked.

✅

Digital Attendance

Give your student ID number at the venue and the host records your attendance instantly. Your history stays saved under My Schedule.

How it works

  1. 1

    Find an event

    Open the Events page and search or filter the directory for an activity you want to join.

  2. 2

    Submit the registration form

    Enter your name, student ID number, and course. Your reservation is confirmed the moment you submit.

  3. 3

    Attend and get verified

    On event day, give your student ID number to the host at the entrance to be marked as attended.

  4. 4

    Track your record

    Check My Schedule anytime to review upcoming reservations and your past attendance history.

For student organizations

Accredited organizations and offices manage their activities through the Host Dashboard. Hosts can publish events, monitor registrations in real time, record attendance at the venue, and review attendance reports afterwards.

The Host Dashboard requires host credentials and is available on desktop computers only.
